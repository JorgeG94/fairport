# The same morning as clear_day, with one player decision in it.
#
# Left alone, tightest-fit puts QFA412 (Heavy) on A2 and keeps A1 -- the only
# Super stand -- free for UAE414. Here the player overrides that at 06:46 and
# parks the Heavy on A1.
#
# Twelve minutes later the A380 vacates the runway with nowhere to go, and
# spends the rest of the session on the runway exit asking for a stand that
# will not come free. One command, one consequence, forty minutes apart.
#
# That is the milestone 1 success criterion in miniature: a run where nothing
# looks obviously wrong still ends badly, and the player can trace it back to a
# single gate assignment.
#
# It is also a regression test. `at 06:46 assign_gate 1 1` is the whole of the
# player input, so the session is reproducible from this file and a seed:
#   fairport scenarios/stand_squeeze.txt --seed 42 --hash

seed 42
max_aircraft 16
load airport data/airports/simple.airport

arrival QFA412 H 240 at 06:45 runway 1
arrival VOZ881 M 170 at 06:52 runway 1
arrival UAE414 S 380 at 06:58 runway 1
arrival JST615 M 186 at 07:04 runway 1

# aircraft 1 (QFA412) onto gate 1 (A1, the Super stand)
at 06:46 assign_gate 1 1

run until 09:00

board
dump stats

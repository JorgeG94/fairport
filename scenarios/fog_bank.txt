# The scenario the design document names, and the milestone 1 payoff.
#
# A normal morning until 08:00, then fog drops visibility to 400 metres --
# below minima, so the declared rates go to zero and the airport accepts
# nothing. What follows is the cascade from design section 6.6, and none of it
# is special-cased anywhere:
#
#   departures cannot push, so they keep their stands
#   arrivals cannot park, so they hold
#   holding burns fuel, so they reach bingo
#   at bingo they divert, permanently, and that is a hard failure
#
# Reopening at 10:00 is not recovery. The stack that built up still has to be
# drained at the capacity that has just been restored, and that drain is the
# rest of the day.
#
# Compare against scenarios/full_day.txt, which is the same traffic and the
# same seed with no weather:
#
#   fairport scenarios/full_day.txt --seed 42   ->  0 diverted
#   fairport scenarios/fog_bank.txt --seed 42   -> 14 diverted

seed 42
max_aircraft 128
load airport data/airports/simple.airport
load schedule data/schedules/day1.toml

at 08:00 set_visibility 400
at 10:00 set_visibility 8000

run until 23:00

dump stats
